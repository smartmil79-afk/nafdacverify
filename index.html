How NaijaVerify Works

1. Search
Anyone can search the database without an account.

2. Scan
Users can scan product barcodes or QR codes.

3. Register
A person must create an account before submitting a counterfeit-product report.

4. Report
The reporter provides product information and evidence.

5. Review
Authorized reviewers examine the report.

6. Official Alert
A user report does not automatically make a product "counterfeit". Official confirmation should come through the appropriate regulatory process.
